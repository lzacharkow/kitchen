Going with the single-bowl workstation. The K-33662 is a different direction from the four double-bowls below — one large bowl with a built-in ledge that carries a cutting board, colander, and drying rack across the sink. It also fits a smaller 33" base cabinet, where the four double-bowls all need 36". Dimensions are from Kohler's spec sheets — see the links at the bottom.

The apron gives roughly 49 sq. in. more open basin area (~17% larger); most of the extra length goes into a bigger small bowl. Same depth.

Each bowl is about 28 sq. in. larger (~12%) — roughly 57 sq. in. more total opening across both bowls, plus slightly deeper.

Bottom line: the apron-front models are the larger shells (35½" × 21¼") and put the extra size into the bowls. All four are 16-gauge stainless, so within this set the decision would have come down to bowl configuration (large/medium vs. 50/50) and mount style (standard undermount vs. farmhouse apron).
