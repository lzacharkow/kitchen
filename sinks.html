Each bowl is about 28 sq. in. larger (~12%) — roughly 57 sq. in. more total opening across both bowls, plus slightly deeper.

Bottom line: the apron-front models are the larger shells (35½" × 21¼") and put the extra size into the bowls. All four are 16-gauge stainless, so the real decision is bowl configuration (large/medium vs. 50/50) and mount style (standard undermount vs. farmhouse apron).
